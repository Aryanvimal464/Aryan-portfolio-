The process

A structured QA workflow.

Quality starts with understanding requirements and continues through validation, reporting, and improvement.

01 / ANALYSE

Understand Requirements

Review requirements, acceptance criteria, and expected application behaviour.

02 / DESIGN

Prepare Test Cases

Create scenarios covering core functionality, edge cases, and negative paths.

03 / EXECUTE

Test & Validate

Execute checks, compare expected and actual results, and record defects.

04 / REPORT

Share Findings

Communicate results clearly and provide evidence to support issue resolution.
